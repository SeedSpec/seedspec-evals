# Authorship outcome rubric

Score every dimension from 0 to 4. A score of 2 means usable but materially incomplete; 4 requires direct evidence. Apply the same dimensions to every evaluation variant.

| ID | Dimension | 0 | 2 | 4 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `target-definition` | Desired end state | The requested outcome remains unclear | The main outcome is understandable but material success conditions are vague | The desired state, affected actors, and recognizable success are precise without prescribing an unnecessary path |
| `obligations-and-boundaries` | Obligations and boundaries | Required behavior, invariants, or forbidden states are missing | Main obligations exist with material boundary gaps | Required behavior, invariants, non-goals, constraints, and forbidden states form a coherent operating boundary |
| `material-ambiguity` | Material ambiguity and authority | Consequential uncertainty is silently invented or hidden | Some uncertainty is surfaced or safely defaulted | Consequential choices trace to author authority or remain explicitly unresolved with their impact explained |
| `source-fidelity` | Source fidelity | Source intent is lost, contradicted, or substantially invented | Primary intent survives with some unsupported detail | Every consequential addition traces to source, explicit answer, or clearly labeled uncertainty |
| `evidence-design` | Evidence and success judgment | Success cannot be observed or verification is presented as outcome proof | Some useful checks exist but scopes or proof limits are unclear | Verification, adoption, operational, and outcome evidence are correctly scoped, with feasible ways to judge applicable success |
| `implementation-freedom` | Useful implementation freedom | The output either dictates an unjustified design or leaves consequential behavior open | Major behavior is bounded but freedom and prescription are uneven | Obligations are tight where outcomes matter while architecture and execution remain free unless intent requires otherwise |
| `agent-ready-handoff` | Agent-ready handoff | An implementing agent must reconstruct the problem | An agent can proceed after material clarification | An independent agent can explain the target, authority, boundaries, resources, uncertainty, and success evidence before planning |
| `reusable-improvement` | Reusable improvement over source | The output adds no reliable value beyond the raw material | It reduces some guesswork but remains fragile or one-off | It materially reduces consequential guesswork and can be adapted without losing intent or confusing verification with success |

Use numeric confidence from 0 to 1. Scores with confidence below 0.7 must name the missing or conflicting evidence.
